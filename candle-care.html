GET THE MOST FROM YOUR CANDLE

Candle Care.

✦

A little care goes a long way. Follow these simple guidelines to help you enjoy your Sassy Belle candle from the first burn to the last.

BEFORE YOU LIGHT

Start with a trim.

Before each burn, check the wick and keep it properly trimmed according to the manufacturer's recommended guidance.

SIMPLE CANDLE CARE

Three steps to a better burn.

01
✂

Trim the Wick

Before lighting your candle, check the wick and trim it to the manufacturer's recommended length.

02
✦

Burn Responsibly

Place your candle on a stable, heat resistant surface and keep the surrounding area clear while the candle is burning.

03
♡

Let It Rest

Allow the candle to cool completely before handling it, moving it, or preparing it for its next use.

LIGHT with INTENTION

THE BURNING EXPERIENCE

Give your candle time.

Candles are meant to be enjoyed. When burning your candle, allow enough time for the wax surface to melt evenly according to the specific instructions provided with your product.

Avoid rushing the process or leaving a burning candle unattended. Always follow the safety instructions included with your particular Sassy Belle product.

CANDLE SAFETY

Please burn responsibly.

Candles are beautiful, but they include an open flame. Please use care every time you burn one.

01

Never Leave Unattended

Do not leave a burning candle unattended.

02

Keep Away From Flammables

Keep the candle away from curtains, paper, fabrics, decorations, and other combustible materials.

03

Keep Away From Children & Pets

Place candles where children and pets cannot reach or knock them over.

04

Know When to Stop

Follow the manufacturer's instructions regarding when the candle should no longer be burned.

05

Keep the Area Clear

Keep the candle on a stable surface with enough space around it while burning.

06

Extinguish With Care

Extinguish the flame carefully and allow the candle to cool before handling it.

BETWEEN BURNS

Treat your candle well.

When your candle isn't being enjoyed, keep it covered if the product includes a lid and store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and excessive heat.

Proper storage can help preserve the fragrance and appearance of your candle between uses.
