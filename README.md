# Sassy Belle Candle Co., LLC

A small business website for Sassy Belle Candle Co., LLC, presenting its candle brand, product information, company story, and customer contact or ordering details.

## Live Website

https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/SassyBelleCandlesCo/

## About This Project

- Brand and company information
- Candle products and featured collections, where available
- Customer information and contact pages

The homepage title currently identifies the project as **Sassy Belle Candle Co., LLC | Poured in the Bluegrass**. Sassy Belle Candle Co., LLC creates artisan luxury candles, Cupcake Minis, wax melts, and home fragrances, hand poured in small batches in the heart of the Bluegrass.
